01 · Overview

What this document is

The Store Manager prototype is the phone-shaped surface of Retina AI: a focused, no-chrome capture flow used by Compass Group store associates to onboard items via barcode and label photography. This system codifies the choices that make that flow feel calm, tactile, and quick — so future screens stay coherent.

Design tenets

  • Phone-first, full-bleed. No surrounding chrome. Content fills the viewport; safe-areas respected.
  • Warm utility. Cream surfaces and an amber primary signal “workwear,” not consumer flash.
  • One job per screen. Each screen has a single primary action, anchored in a bottom action bar.
  • Quiet motion. Subtle scanlines, pulses and toasts confirm progress without stealing focus.

Where this applies

  • Routes under /store-manager/* in the Retina app.
  • Screens: Login, SSO, Articles, Capture (Barcode → Front → Back → More), Review, Progress, Done, Retry, Mark Irrelevant, Account.
  • The shared StoreManagerShell + StoreManagerLayout wrappers and the toast/context provider.

Logo & mark

RETINA AI
by Compass Group

Mark anatomy

A compass diagonal — needle and counter-arrow — set on a rounded amber tile. The diagonal references both navigation (Compass Group) and the act of capturing an item from corner to corner.

Wordmark

RETINA set in Inter Bold, paired with a faint-grey AI to keep the AI signifier present but quiet. Tagline “by Compass Group” always uppercase, tracked 0.18em, in #9C9B94.

Minimum size

Mark: 44 px minimum on screen. Wordmark + tagline lockup: 120 px wide minimum.

Brand voice in the mark

Warm

Amber over cream evokes paper, packaging, retail backrooms — not a sci-fi scanner.

Precise

The diagonal is hairline; corners are crisp. Implies measurement, not branding flourish.

Operational

The tagline carries the parent brand quietly. The app is a tool, not a marketing surface.

Pairing rules

  • Titles use Ink Primary (#2B2A26). Labels above icons use Ink Muted.

  • Number-heavy data (GTIN, percent, counts) → JetBrains Mono, never Inter.

Buttons, pills & chips

Buttons

Primary

#C68A1E fill, white text, 8 px radius, 48 px min-height. One per screen. Always pinned to the bottom action bar with border-top: 1px solid #F1ECDD.

Secondary

White fill, 1 px #C5C4BC border, ink-primary text. Pairs with a primary in a 0.55 / 1 flex ratio inside the action bar.

Opacity 0.55, pointer-events: none. Don’t grey out — keep amber so the next available action remains visually present.

Capture surfaces

The capture screen is the heart of the prototype. It composes four distinct surfaces: a light sub-bar, a dark camera stage, a slim quality bar, a horizontal thumbnail row, and a pinned action bar.

Camera stage

Barcode framing

230×118 frame on a #080808 stage with a radial vignette. Green #97C459 corner markers (2.5 px) and a scanline that travels top→bottom on a 2.2 s ease (cubic-bezier(0.45, 0, 0.55, 1)).

Point at front label

Label framing

For Front / Back / More: a 180×220 dashed frame in 35% white. Hint text in 45% white. No scanline — only the barcode step uses the green markers.

12 · Voice & tone

How the app speaks

The user is on a clock, often standing, often interrupted. Copy is short, specific to the moment, and avoids both apology and celebration.

Microcopy principles

  • Verbs first. “Capture front”, “Retry barcode”, “Mark irrelevant”.
  • Name what was captured. Toasts say “Barcode captured”, not “Done”.
  • Counts over percentages. “3 / 4 captured”, not “75% done”.
  • One sentence ceiling. Two only on error screens that need a remedy.

Vocabulary

  • Article — a product the manager is onboarding.
  • Capture — taking one of barcode / front / back / more.
  • Mark irrelevant — soft-deletes an article from this session.
  • SSO — Microsoft Entra ID; never expand inline.
  • GTIN — always uppercase, mono font.

Do & Don’t

Do “Front label captured” → toast on success.
“Point at front label” → hint inside the camera frame.
“Retry — image was blurry” → specific remedy.
Don’t “Great job! ✨” — performative.
“Something went wrong.” — generic and unactionable.
“Click here to continue.” — desktop language, vague verb.

End-of-flow language

  • Done screen — “Items submitted. You can capture another.” Provide one secondary “Back to Articles” link.
  • Retry screen — name the failed capture step, offer “Retake” as the primary action, “Skip” as secondary only when truly optional.
  • Mark Irrelevant — confirm with a single line: “Removed from this session.” No undo toast; the article re-appears in the list with a strike-through.
This system is alive. When you add a screen, name the pattern it follows (A–D) and the tokens it borrows. If a new pattern emerges, propose it as a section before shipping — coherence is a feature.
